The man on the coin

Anicius Manlius Severinus Boethius. Roman senator, consul in 510, adviser to Theodoric, the Ostrogothic king of Italy.

In 523 the king turned on him. Accused of treason, he was thrown in prison to wait for his execution. There, with nothing left, he wrote The Consolation of Philosophy.

Rotam volubili orbe versamus, infima summis, summa infimis mutare gaudemus.

We turn the wheel in its spinning circle, and love to bring the lowest to the top and the top to the bottom. Fortune, in the Consolation, book II

He was executed in 524. The book was copied, translated and read for a thousand years. Chaucer put it into English and called it Boece. That is the name on the coin.
